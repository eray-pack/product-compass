## 🪲 Bug on the tree — mic interaction
The tree page goes idle → a bug flies in and lands on the tree. A popup appears saying "blow on it." The mic activates (like iPhone voice command), detects the blow sound, and the bug animates flying off the tree.

**Why it's interesting:** turns a passive screen into a physical moment. Feels alive and unexpected.  
**When to revisit:** after tree interactions are solid and the app is in TestFlight.
